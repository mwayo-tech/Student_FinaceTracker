About

Student Finance Tracker helps you record and understand your day-to-day spending while at school. Track transactions, stay within your monthly budget, and search your history using plain text or regex patterns.

Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no third-party dependencies. All data is stored locally in your browser.

Features: regex-powered search, live validation, budget progress, 7-day trend chart, JSON import/export, multi-currency settings, and full keyboard accessibility.
